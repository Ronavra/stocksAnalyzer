# Research Engine Specification

Each weekly company snapshot must distinguish observed facts from interpretation.

## Inputs
1. Price/market data
2. Financial statements and company filings
3. Earnings results, guidance and estimate revisions
4. Company/sector news
5. Macro and geopolitical context
6. Market sentiment

## Required analysis
- What actually happened?
- Why may investors care?
- Is the likely effect short or long term?
- Which revenue, margin, EPS, cash-flow or balance-sheet metric could change?
- Which assumptions appear embedded in expectations/valuation?
- Bull, base and bear scenarios
- Upcoming catalysts
- Risks / thesis invalidators
- Change versus prior weekly snapshot

## Initial signal dimensions
Fundamentals, valuation, earnings/revisions, momentum, news/sentiment and catalysts.

Scores are research prioritization signals. They are not personalized buy/sell instructions.
